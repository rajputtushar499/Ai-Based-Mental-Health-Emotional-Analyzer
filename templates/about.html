About the project

Project title

AI-Based Mental Health Emotion Analyzer

Objective

To provide an AI-assisted platform that analyzes emotional signals from text and speech and provides supportive wellness insights.

How it works

  1. Text: cleaned text goes to a pretrained DistilBERT emotion classifier. Its six base emotions are mapped to seven app emotions; stress and anxiety are derived with a documented word-cue rule.
  2. Speech: Librosa extracts MFCC, mel spectrogram, zero-crossing rate and chroma features, and a pretrained wav2vec2 model predicts the emotion from the audio.
  3. Support: a rule-based engine returns general wellness tips and a gentle alert if recent results are consistently strongly negative.

Limitations

Emotion models are imperfect, trained mostly on English text and acted speech. Results are a rough screening-style signal and never a diagnosis. {{ disclaimer }}
